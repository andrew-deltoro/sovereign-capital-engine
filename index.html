Screener — Ranked Candidates

Score out of 100 = Trend 35 (four moving-average tests) + Momentum 25 (RSI band 15, 20-day return 10) + Liquidity 20 (avg dollar volume) + Risk 20 (inverse volatility 12, inverse drawdown 8). Strong Watch means 75+ with acceptable liquidity — it is a flag to look closer, not a signal to buy. These weights are a judgement call with no backtest behind them, and every rule here is one that thousands of others also run. Do your own work before acting.

Crypto Screener — Ranked Candidates

Score /100 = Momentum 40 (24h 12 + 7d 15 + 30d 13) + Liquidity 30 + Market cap 15 + Risk 15. Different model from equities because the useful inputs differ. Crypto momentum mean-reverts hard — a coin scoring 90 has usually already made its move. Stablecoins are excluded. Data from CoinMarketCap, top 200 by market cap.

Range is estimated. CoinMarketCap's free tier has no OHLCV, so the 24h high/low the Risk bucket needs is inferred from the 1h and 24h percentage moves rather than measured. Risk scores are therefore approximate, and the estimate runs slightly wide — treat the Risk column as a rough guide, not a number to act on.
