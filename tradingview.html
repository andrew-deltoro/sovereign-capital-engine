Pre-Deploy Checklist

  • Is the overall market risk-on or risk-off?
  • Is the asset liquid enough to enter and exit?
  • Is the trend clean?
  • Is there a catalyst or reason for movement?
  • Is the stop logical, not arbitrary?
  • Is the position size small enough to survive a loss?
  • Is earnings or a major event coming?
  • Is the asset overextended?
  • Am I already overexposed to this sector or asset class?
  • Would I still take this trade if I lost the last three trades?
